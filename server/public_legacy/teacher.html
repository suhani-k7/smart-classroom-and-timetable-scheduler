Select a faculty profile from the current dataset to view their expertise, configure unavailable slots, and inspect their personal timetable.

Availability Legend (Hard Constraint H4)
  • ☑ Checked UNAVAILABLE: Faculty cannot teach during this day/slot. The scheduler will never assign a class here.
  • ☐ Unchecked AVAILABLE: Faculty is eligible and available to be scheduled if needed.
⚠️ Institutional Schedule Impact

Marking slots unavailable constrains the entire timetable. When regenerated, the solver re-optimizes assignments across all sections, rooms, and faculty to satisfy constraints H1–H8. Other teachers' or sections' sessions may shift as a result.
